# Customer Shopping Behavior Analysis

## 📌 Project Overview

This project analyzes customer shopping behavior using transactional data from **3,900 purchases across multiple product categories**. The objective is to identify customer spending patterns, product preferences, subscription trends, and purchasing behavior to support data-driven business decisions.

The project follows an end-to-end data analytics workflow, including data cleaning and exploratory data analysis in Python, business analysis using PostgreSQL, and interactive dashboard development in Power BI.

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- Analyze customer purchasing and spending patterns.
- Compare revenue across customer demographics and age groups.
- Evaluate the impact of discounts and promotional offers on purchases.
- Understand subscription behavior and customer loyalty.
- Identify top-rated and best-selling products.
- Build an interactive dashboard to communicate business insights.
- Provide actionable recommendations to improve customer retention and business performance.

## 📊 Dataset Description

The dataset contains customer demographics, purchase transactions, product information, and shopping behavior.

- **Total records:** 3,900
- **Total columns:** 18
- **Missing values:** 37 in the `review_rating` column

| Category | Attributes |

| Customer Demographics | Age, Gender, Location |
| Subscription Information | Subscription Status |
| Purchase Details | Item Purchased, Category, Purchase Amount |

## 🔄 Project Workflow

### 1. Data Cleaning and Exploratory Data Analysis — Python

The dataset was loaded and analyzed using Pandas.

Key activities:
- Inspected dataset structure using `df.info()`.
- Generated descriptive statistics using `df.describe()`.
- Identified missing values and handled 37 missing review ratings using the median rating of each product category.
- Standardized column names using snake_case.
- Created an `age_group` feature by grouping customer ages.
- Created a `purchase_frequency_days` feature from purchase data.
- Checked for redundancy between discount and promo code columns and removed `promo_code_used`.
- Connected Python to PostgreSQL and loaded the cleaned DataFrame into the database.

### 2. Business Analysis — PostgreSQL and SQL

SQL queries were developed to answer ten key business questions:

1. **Revenue by Gender:** Compared total revenue generated by male and female customers.
2. **High-Spending Discount Users:** Identified customers who used discounts but spent above the average purchase amount.
3. **Top 5 Products by Rating:** Identified products with the highest average review ratings.
4. **Shipping Type Comparison:** Compared average purchase amounts for Standard and Express shipping.
5. **Subscribers vs. Non-Subscribers:** Compared average spending and total revenue by subscription status.
6. **Discount-Dependent Products:** Identified five products with the highest percentage of discounted purchases.
7. **Customer Segmentation:** Classified customers as New, Returning, or Loyal based on purchase history.
8. **Top 3 Products per Category:** Identified the most purchased products within each product category.
9. **Repeat Buyers and Subscriptions:** Examined whether customers with more than five purchases were more likely to subscribe.
10. **Revenue by Age Group:** Calculated the revenue contribution of different customer age groups.

### 3. Interactive Dashboard — Power BI

An interactive Power BI dashboard was developed to visualize the shopping behavior analysis and present business insights in an accessible format.

The dashboard supports the exploration of customer purchasing patterns, revenue distribution, product preferences, and subscription behavior.

## 💡 Key Business Recommendations

Based on the analysis objectives, the following recommendations were identified:

- **Boost Subscriptions:** Promote exclusive benefits and incentives to encourage customers to subscribe.
- **Improve Customer Loyalty:** Introduce loyalty programs and rewards to encourage repeat purchases and move customers into the Loyal segment.
- **Optimize Discount Policies:** Evaluate discount effectiveness while maintaining profitability and controlling margins.
- **Improve Product Positioning:** Highlight highly rated and best-selling products in marketing campaigns.
- **Use Targeted Marketing:** Focus marketing efforts on high-revenue age groups and customers associated with Express shipping.

The findings and recommendations can help businesses better understand customer behavior, evaluate promotional strategies, improve customer retention, and make informed marketing and product decisions.
